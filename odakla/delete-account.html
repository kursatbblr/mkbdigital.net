Delete your Odakla account

You can delete your account directly in the Odakla app from Profile/Settings → Delete Account.

If you cannot access the app, contact support@mkbdigital.net from the email associated with your Odakla account and request account deletion.

When your account is deleted, your Odakla account and associated server-side data are removed, except information we may be required to retain for legal, security, fraud prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

Local app data stored on your device may also be removed when you delete the account or uninstall the app.
